01 About the artist

I chase light, weather and silence — one quiet frame at a time.

0 Frames
shared

Hello, I'm Krishnendu Dutta (@ikrishnendu_dutta) — a self-taught nature photographer from Kolkata, India. I share the world as I see it through my lens: golden hours, quiet forests, distant peaks, and the small wild lives that share our planet.

Photography, for me, is a practice of slowing down. I am at the beginning of this journey — learning, exploring, and growing with every frame. This is my growing archive of moments that moved me. Thank you for being here.
